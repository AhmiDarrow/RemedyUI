# 17. 3D

## 3D in effects

Prism ([16](16-effects.md)) draws 3D procedurally, inside an element: cameras
(`look_at`, `camera_ray`, `project`), signed-distance shapes and operators, raymarching with
fixed step counts, normals, soft shadows, ambient occlusion, GGX shading, fog, volumes.
Examples: `orb.rfx`, `crystal.rfx`, `volumetric_clouds.rfx`. Safe for strangers and on both
renderers; costs are bounded at compile time.

Good for: decorative 3D, hero art, theme centrepieces. Not for: meshes, models, interaction
with 3D objects, 3D placement of UI.
